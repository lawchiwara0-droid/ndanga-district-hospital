About Ndanga District Hospital

This website is intended to make approved public information about the hospital easier for patients, visitors and the community to find.

Ndanga District Hospital serves people in Ndanga, Zaka District and the surrounding area. A fuller description of the hospital's history, catchment area and role will be added once confirmed by management.
